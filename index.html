Local Data Insights (Romania–Moldova)

Structured data on local markets for market analysis, client discovery, and digital visibility assessment.

What LDI is

Local Data Insights (LDI) turns publicly available digital information about local businesses into structured, analysis-ready datasets.

These datasets reflect the visible local market: where businesses are located, how a market is distributed, how concentrated competition is, and where weak digital presence may create commercial opportunities.

What LDI is used for

LDI helps replace manual search with structured market data. It can be used for client discovery, competition analysis, market entry decisions, and identifying businesses with limited or missing digital presence.

This makes it relevant for digital and marketing agencies, B2B service providers, suppliers, distributors, SaaS products, operators, and investors exploring local markets.

What is included in the datasets

Depending on the dataset, records may include business name, category, city or local area, geographic coordinates, website availability, phone, email, observable social media presence, and Google Maps reference.

What makes LDI different

LDI is not just a list of companies. It creates a structured digital layer of the market that helps reveal market structure, business visibility, and commercial gaps in local ecosystems.
