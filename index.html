Data Transparency and Limits

LDI emphasizes transparency, verifiability, and clear separation between observable data and official sources.

  • Each dataset has a clear snapshot date.
  • It's clear which fields are included in the data.
  • It's clear what the data can and cannot show.
  • Data reflects the visible market in public digital sources, not an official registry.
  • Public samples and dashboards allow evaluation of the data structure before use.

LDI data does not represent an official registry. It reflects a structured view of the visible market from public digital sources.
