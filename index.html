Local Data Insights (Romania–Moldova)

Structured data on local markets for client discovery, market analysis, and understanding the digital presence of businesses.

What LDI is

Local Data Insights (LDI) turns publicly available digital information about local businesses into structured datasets.

These datasets reflect both market structure and the observable digital presence of businesses. They help show where businesses are located, how a market is distributed, where competition is concentrated, and where digital gaps may create commercial opportunities.

What LDI is used for

LDI helps move from manual search to working with structured data. It can be used for client discovery, competition analysis, market entry decisions, and identifying businesses with weak or missing digital presence.

This makes it useful for digital and marketing agencies, B2B service providers, suppliers, distributors, SaaS products, business operators, and investors exploring local markets.

What is included in the datasets

Depending on the category and market, datasets may include business name, category, city or area, geographic coordinates, website availability, phone, email, observable social media presence, and Google Maps reference.

What makes LDI different

LDI is not just a list of companies. It is a structured digital layer of the market that shows where businesses are located, how visible they are online, and where digital gaps create real commercial opportunities.
