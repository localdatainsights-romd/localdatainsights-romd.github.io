Local Data Insights

Structured data about local markets for client discovery, analysis, and understanding the digital presence of businesses.

What Local Data Insights is

Local Data Insights (LDI) is a project that transforms publicly available digital information about local businesses into structured datasets.

The data is collected, cleaned, and normalized to create datasets that reflect both the composition of the market and the level of digital presence of businesses.

What LDI provides

LDI enables a shift from manual search to working with structured data, making it possible to quickly identify opportunities within a market.

  • Understand market structure and distribution
  • Analyze competition and segment density
  • Identify businesses with limited digital presence
  • Find potential clients for services and products
  • Explore local markets based on data, not assumptions

Who this is useful for

  • Digital and marketing agencies
  • B2B service providers
  • SaaS products and platforms
  • Suppliers and distributors
  • Entrepreneurs and operators
  • Investors exploring local markets

What is included in the datasets

  • Business name
  • Category
  • City and area
  • Geographic coordinates (lat / lon)
  • Website (availability and link)
  • Phone and email (where available)
  • Social media (Facebook, Instagram, etc.)
  • Google Maps reference

Where it can be used

  • Client discovery and lead generation
  • Market segmentation
  • Competitive analysis
  • Market entry planning
  • Identifying digital gaps
  • Local market intelligence

Example of use

When analyzing a local market (e.g., dentistry, fitness, or beauty), LDI allows you to quickly understand:

  • How many businesses operate in the market
  • How they are distributed geographically
  • The density of competition
  • Which businesses lack a website or strong digital presence

This makes it possible not only to observe the market, but to identify where services or products can be offered.

What makes LDI different

LDI is not just a list of companies.

It is a structured digital layer of the market that shows:

  • Where businesses are located
  • How the market is distributed
  • Where competition is concentrated
  • Where digital opportunities exist

Formats

  • Ready-to-use datasets by category and city
  • Sample datasets
  • Custom selections for specific needs
  • (Planned) analytical reports
