Dental Clinics in Cluj (Romania)

This page provides a structured dataset of dental clinics in Cluj, Romania.

This dataset covers Cluj-Napoca and surrounding urban areas within Cluj, reflecting the broader regional dental market rather than a single city.

Snapshot information

Identified clinics: 410
Latest full snapshot: January 2026
Updates are performed periodically based on the data processing cycle.

How many dental clinics are in Cluj?

This dataset captures the visible structure of the dental services market in Cluj based on publicly available digital sources.

Data is collected from publicly visible digital business listings and normalized using the LDI processing pipeline.

Each entity is identified, verified, and normalized. The dataset includes geographic coordinates and a direct map reference, allowing validation and spatial analysis without additional research.

The count does not represent an official exhaustive registry, but a structured aggregation of operators visible in the public digital ecosystem.

Market structure inside Cluj

  • Cluj-Napoca: 341 clinics
  • Florești: 36 clinics
  • Turda: 25 clinics
  • Câmpia Turzii: 8 clinics

More than 80% of all clinics are concentrated in Cluj-Napoca, with surrounding towns forming a secondary layer of the regional market.

What is included in the dental clinic dataset
