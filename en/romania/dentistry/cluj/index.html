This page provides a structured dataset of dental clinics in Cluj, Romania, based on publicly available digital sources.

The dataset reflects both the structure of the local market and the observable digital presence of businesses.

Snapshot information

How many dental clinics are in Cluj?

This dataset captures the visible structure of the dental services market in Cluj at a specific point in time.

Data is collected from publicly available digital business sources and normalized using the LDI processing pipeline.

The count reflects businesses visible in the public digital ecosystem and does not represent an official registry.

How the data is structured

Each entity is identified, verified, and normalized.

The dataset includes geographic coordinates and map references, allowing spatial validation and analysis.

What is included in the dataset

  • Business name
  • Physical address
  • Geographic coordinates (latitude, longitude)
  • Direct map reference
  • Website (where available)
  • Contact information (phone, email where available)
  • Social media presence (where available)
  • Normalized category
