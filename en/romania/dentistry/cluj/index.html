Dental Clinics in Cluj (Romania)

Romanian version

This page presents a structured dataset of dental clinics in Cluj, Romania, based on publicly available digital sources.

It shows the local market structure and the observable digital presence of businesses.

Snapshot

Dental clinics identified: 410
Latest snapshot: January 2026
Updates are performed periodically.

The dataset reflects the visible local market and does not represent an official registry.

What is included in the dataset

The dataset brings together business identity, location, and available digital presence information in one structured view of the local market.

  • Business name
  • Physical address
  • Geographic coordinates (latitude, longitude)
  • Direct map reference
  • Website (where available)
  • Contact information (phone, email where available)
  • Social media presence (where available)
  • Normalized category
