Dental Clinics in Mures (Romania)

This page presents a structured dataset of dental providers in Mures, Romania, built from publicly available digital sources.

It reflects the local market structure and the observable digital presence of dental providers.

How many dental clinics are in Mures?

This snapshot includes 193 dental providers in Mures, based on publicly available digital sources.
Latest snapshot: January 2026

This reflects the visible local market and does not represent an official registry.

What is included in the dataset

The dataset brings together business identity, location, contact details, and available digital presence information in one structured view of the local market.

  • Business name
  • Physical address
  • Geographic coordinates (latitude, longitude)
  • Direct map reference
  • Website (where available)
  • Contact information (phone, email where available)
  • Social media presence (where available)
  • Normalized category
