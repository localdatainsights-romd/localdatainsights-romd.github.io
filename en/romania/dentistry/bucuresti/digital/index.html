Interpretation

These indicators show differences in visible digital presence across the market.

Some providers appear with a more complete visible online presence in this dataset, while others appear with a more limited footprint across websites, contact channels, and social media.

What these indicators are based on

  • Publicly available business listings
  • Observed website presence
  • Available contact information
  • Detected social media profiles

How these indicators can be used

  • Exploring website and social media presence
  • Understanding differences in visible digital presence
  • Identifying segments with lower online visibility
  • Supporting market analysis and client discovery
