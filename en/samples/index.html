What You Can Check in a Sample

A sample does not replace the full dataset but allows you to verify how the data is structured and presented.

Field Structure

You can see how fields are organized: business name, category, location, website, contacts, and other available information.

Data Format

Samples help verify compatibility with existing workflows: spreadsheet analysis, filtering, import, or local exploration.

Utility for Analysis

You can assess whether the data structure is suitable for prospecting, market analysis, digital audit, or geo-analysis.

What a Full Dataset Can Include

  • Business name and normalized category.
  • Address, city, county, and location fields.
  • Geographic coordinates, where available.
  • Website, phone, email, and observable digital channels.
  • Social media, where available.
  • Digital presence indicators.
  • Snapshot date and notes about data limitations.

The exact structure may vary depending on vertical, city, county, and the availability of public digital sources.
