What You Can Evaluate in a Sample

A sample does not replace the full dataset but allows you to evaluate how the data is structured and whether it fits your needs.

Field Structure

You can assess how fields are organized: business name, category, location, website, contacts, and other available information.

Data Format

Samples help you verify compatibility with your workflows, such as spreadsheet analysis, filtering, import, or local exploration.

Utility for Analysis

Evaluate whether the data structure is suitable for prospecting, market analysis, digital audit, or geo-analysis.

What a Full Dataset Includes

  • Business name and normalized category (standardized for consistency).
  • Address, city, county, and other location-related fields.
  • Geographic coordinates (latitude and longitude), where available.
  • Website, phone number, email, and observable digital channels.
  • Public social media profiles, where available.
  • Digital presence indicators (such as website visibility, activity on social media, etc.).
  • Snapshot date and notes about data limitations (e.g., missing data or sources).

The exact structure may vary depending on vertical, city, county, and the availability of public digital sources.
