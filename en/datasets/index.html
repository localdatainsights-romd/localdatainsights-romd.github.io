What LDI Datasets Include

The exact structure may vary depending on the vertical and data availability, but LDI datasets are designed for use in analysis, market exploration, and geo-analysis.

Business Identity

Business name, category, vertical, city, county, and other classification fields, depending on available data.

Location and Coordinates

Address, city, county, latitude, longitude, and direct map link where these data are available.

Contact and Digital Channels

Website, phone, email, social media, and other observable digital channels.

Digital Indicators

Signals such as the presence of a website, contact data availability, and social media presence.

Working Formats

Datasets are provided in CSV + XLSX formats for analysis, filtering, and integration into existing workflows.

Snapshot Date

Each dataset is associated with a snapshot date to avoid confusion between historical, current, and future data.

Extended Coverage

LDI gradually extends coverage to cities, counties, and verticals. Publishing depends on data availability, source quality, and validation capacity.
