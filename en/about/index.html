About Local Data Insights

Local Data Insights (LDI) is a startup based in Moldova, focused on Romania and Moldova. We transform open digital information about local businesses into structured datasets, helping companies analyze and improve their digital presence.

Our mission is to simplify access to data for small and medium-sized businesses, marketers, and investors by providing the information needed for more informed decisions in local competitive markets.

What We Are Building

This is only the first stage of our project. We continue to grow and improve the quality of our data so we can provide more accurate and more useful datasets over time.

Our current priority markets are Romania and Moldova, with a focus on structured local business data and digital coverage.

We are always open to new partnerships and collaboration opportunities with companies that can use our data for business growth and better working processes.
