What You Can Request

A custom dataset can be created for a specific local market if sufficient publicly available data exists to build a useful and verifiable dataset.

Another City or County

For example, a customized dataset for a specific city, county, or urban area in Romania.

Another Vertical

A specific category of local businesses, if it can be identified, classified, and structured based on available sources.

Custom Selection

A customized list based on criteria such as location, category, website, contact channels, or observable digital presence.

When a Custom Dataset is Useful

Local Prospecting

For identifying relevant local businesses for B2B services, digital agencies, or suppliers.

Market Analysis

To analyze the visible structure of a local market: the number of businesses, their locations, and their digital presence.

Geo-analysis

For geographic analysis, territorial comparisons, and local business distribution, where location data is available.

What to Include in Your Request

To speed up the evaluation process, please provide as much detail as possible about the type of data you need and its intended use.

  • Country, city, county, or area of interest.
  • Business category or desired industry vertical.
  • Purpose: prospecting, market analysis, digital audit, geo-analysis, or other specific uses.
  • Key fields for the project: address, coordinates, website, phone, email, social media, and others.
  • Estimated volume or level of detail required, if available.

Please send these details to localdatainsights@gmail.com.

How the Process Works

1. Submit Your Request

Describe the data you need, including the vertical, geography, and purpose for which you need the data.

2. We Assess Availability

We assess the availability of sufficient public data and confirm whether it can be structured into a usable format.

3. We Define the Format

If the request is feasible, we define the necessary fields, geography, format, and verification requirements, and provide an estimated timeline.
