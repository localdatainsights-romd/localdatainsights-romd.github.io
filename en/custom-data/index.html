What You Can Request

A custom dataset can be built for a specific local market if there is enough publicly available data to create a useful and verifiable dataset.

Another City or County

For example, a local selection for a specific city, county, or urban area in Romania.

Another Vertical

A specific category of local businesses, if it can be identified and structured based on available sources.

Custom Selection

A structured list based on criteria such as location, category, website, contact channels, or observable digital presence.

When a Custom Dataset is Useful

Local Prospecting

To identify relevant local businesses for B2B services, digital agencies, suppliers, or other commercial activities.

Market Analysis

To understand the visible structure of a local market: how many actors there are, where they are located, and what digital channels they use.

Geo-analysis

For maps, territorial comparisons, and exploring the local distribution of businesses, where location data is available.

What to Include in Your Request

For faster evaluation, provide as much clarity as possible regarding the type of data you need and how you intend to use it.

  • Country, city, county, or area of interest.
  • Business category or desired vertical.
  • Purpose of use: prospecting, market analysis, digital audit, geo-analysis, or other purposes.
  • Important fields for the project: address, coordinates, website, phone, email, social media, etc.
  • Estimated volume or level of detail required, if known.

You can send these details to localdatainsights@gmail.com.

How the Process Works

1. Send Your Request

Describe the vertical, geography, and purpose for which you need data.

2. We Evaluate Availability

We check if sufficient public data exists and whether it can be structured into a usable format.

3. We Define the Format

If the request is feasible, we define the fields, geography, format, and level of verification required.

Limitations

LDI does not promise full coverage for any category or city. Data depends on the publicly available digital sources and the ability to structure and verify the data. LDI datasets reflect the visible market and do not represent an official registry.
